The ten questions

  1. How many people touch content before it ships? Name the roles, not the names.
  2. What ships in a typical month? (emails, pages, posts, campaigns, videos: rough counts)
  3. What tools does the work actually live in today? Not the ones you bought, the ones people open.
  4. How does new work arrive right now? Walk us through the last request you received, start to finish.
  5. Who has to say yes before something ships, and where do they say it?
  6. What shipped late most recently? What was the actual holdup, best guess?
  7. Where do finished files live, and how long does it take to find one from three months ago?
  8. What does urgent do to your process? Skip steps? Which ones?
  9. If one part of this fixed itself overnight, which part would you pick?
  10. Anything you already tried that did not stick? What killed it?

The three screenshots. This is where the truth lives.

  • A. Your project board, tracker, or list as it looks right now. Do not tidy it first. The mess is the data.
  • B. One real brief or request, forwarded or screenshotted, exactly as it arrived.
  • C. The folder where finished work lives.

What you get back, within three business days

  • A written diagnosis: the two or three places your workflow actually breaks, mapped from what you sent, not from a generic checklist.
  • The recommended system: which parts of our standard build fix which of your breaks, in what order.
  • A fixed quote for the 30-Day Build. Your $900 credits toward it in full if you proceed within 60 days.
  • No call at any point, unless you want one.
