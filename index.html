Fair questions

The things you are actually wondering.

Why is there no sales call?

Because your time is worth something and so is ours, and a free consultation is usually a sales pitch with better manners. The Diagnosis is a fixed thing at a fixed price. The Build gets quoted in writing, so the first conversation we have is one where we are already working for you.

What happens after I buy the Diagnosis?

Within one business day you get the survey: ten questions and three screenshots, including your project board exactly as it looks right now. Do not tidy it first; the mess is the data. Within three business days of your answers, you get the written diagnosis, the recommended system, and a fixed Build quote.

Do we have to switch tools?

No. We build in whatever you already use: Notion, Asana, Monday, Workfront, a shared drive. New software is usually how these projects die.

Who does the work?

A deliberately small team. Someone senior runs your engagement from start to finish, and specialists come in for specific builds when needed. Nothing gets handed to a junior coordinator.

What happens when you leave?

The system keeps running. Everything is documented in plain English, your team is trained on it, and it lives in your accounts. If it only worked while we were in the room, we would have sold you a dependency instead of a system.

Can you stay on long-term?

After a Build, yes, month to month. But the system is designed so you will not need us, and we would rather say that up front.

In rough water, a ship steers by a fixed point. Something steady, that says: this is the way through.
